# Fauna sprites

Pixel-art "Animated Wild Animals" by **ScratchIO**, released under **CC0** (public domain).
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/animated-wild-animals

Each file is a horizontal strip of equal frames. Walk and idle for six animals:
bear, boar, deer, fox, rabbit (hop + idle), wolf (walk + howl, the howl is its "idle").
The frame sizes and counts live in `js/fauna.js`. The sprites face right; the site flips them with CSS.

CC0 needs no credit, but they are credited in CREDITS.md and the page footer anyway.

## Newer animals (cat, dog, squirrel)

- `cat-*.png`: "Cat sprites" by Shepardskin, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/cat-sprites
- `dog-*.png`: "Dog Sprites" by Shepardskin, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/dog-sprites
- `squirrel-*.png`: "Pixel Squirrel", CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-squirrel

The originals are GIF animations. I cut their frames into strips (same format as the others), removed the solid background colour, and mirrored cat and squirrel so everything faces right. The pixels were not changed.
