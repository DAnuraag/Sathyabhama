# Your photos

Put your pictures in this folder (jpg, jpeg, png, webp, avif, gif). That's all.
They appear in the "Us, so far" section on their own, in file-name order (name them 01.jpg, 02.jpg … to control the order).
Tap a photo on the site to view it larger (arrow keys / Esc work).

- No pictures here = the whole section stays hidden.
- Captions are optional: `photoCaptions` in `config.js`, e.g. `'01.jpg': 'The day we ...'`.
- Phone photos are big. For a fast page, export them around 1600 px on the long side (about 300 to 600 KB each).
